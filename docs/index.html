Methodology & notes. Data from the EIA Short-Term Energy Outlook (STEO), monthly, at basin/region granularity; values beyond the latest actual month are EIA forecasts (shaded). Crude and tight oil in million bbl/day; new-well and base-change series in thousand bbl/day; gas in Bcf/day. Well-level decline curves, water cut, and gas-oil ratio require well-level data (Texas RRC / New Mexico OCD) and are out of scope at this granularity. Built with Python (ETL via GitHub Actions) and Plotly.
